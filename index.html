Our mission

A different kind of internship

The job market for CS graduates has changed. Resumes that mirror job descriptions are filtered by algorithms before a human ever reads them. BlockLaunch exists to change what a graduate brings to the table.

🎓

Built for CofO students

College of the Ozarks students work through the summer to cover room and board. Traditional internships aren't financially viable. BlockLaunch pays for deliverables — on your schedule, around your job.

🔗

Real blockchain projects

Work on stablecoins, tokenized deposits, DeFi protocols, and financial infrastructure — the same problems being solved at the frontier of fintech. Not toy apps. Actual software.

⚡

Your IP, always

Everything you build belongs to you. BlockLaunch gets a license to use it, but you can publish it, open-source it, license it, and put it on your resume the day you finish.

💳

Scholarship, not payroll

Compensation is structured as a restricted scholarship through College of the Ozarks — no W-2, no employment paperwork, no summer tradeoffs. The college handles disbursement.

For students

How it works

From proposal to shipped software in one summer — without leaving your campus job.

1

Submit a project proposal

Propose a blockchain or fintech project, or choose from BlockLaunch's open project briefs. We'll scope it together into clear, achievable deliverables.

2

Get sponsored

BlockLaunch funds the scholarship through CofO. You receive support to cover the financial gap left by not taking a traditional summer job.

3

Build on your schedule

Work on deliverables around your campus job — evenings, weekends, whenever. No office hours, no time tracking. Ship the thing, that's all that matters.

4

Graduate with a portfolio

Your finished project is public on GitHub under your name. A live URL, real code, modern tech. That's your resume now.

Technology focus

What we're building

BlockLaunch explores the intersection of blockchain infrastructure and everyday financial products — the same space community banks and credit unions are navigating right now.

Stablecoins & tokenized deposits

How do community financial institutions participate in on-chain settlement? Projects explore the rails being built between traditional banking and blockchain networks.

DeFi protocol integration

Building consumer-facing interfaces on top of existing DeFi infrastructure — lending, liquidity, cross-chain bridging — with an emphasis on real utility over speculation.

Financial tooling & APIs

Modernizing the interfaces that community banks use to manage accounts, payments, and reporting — applying modern API design and cloud infrastructure to legacy systems.
