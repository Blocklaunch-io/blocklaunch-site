How it works

From idea to shipped software

BlockLaunch is structured around deliverables, not hours. You set your own schedule and work at your own pace — what matters is that you ship.

1

Identify a problem worth solving

Propose a project or choose from open BlockLaunch briefs. Every project must address a real gap — something that exists better or differently than what's currently available. We'll scope it into clear, achievable deliverables together.

2

Get sponsored

Accepted projects receive funding to cover your time. Compensation is structured to remove financial barriers — so you can focus on building without compromise.

3

Build with modern tools

You'll work with a real tech stack — cloud infrastructure, blockchain protocols, modern frameworks, and AI tooling. BlockLaunch provides mentorship, access to tooling, and a collaborative environment to help you level up fast.

4

Ship it publicly

Your finished project is live, public, and yours. A GitHub repo with real code, a deployed product with a real URL, and professional experience at BlockLaunch LLC on your resume — that's how you stand out.
